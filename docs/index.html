Pentair PG3x Plugin

A Universal Devices eisy / PG3x plugin for supported Pentair connected pool equipment.

Pentair CloudPG3xIoXAutomatic Discovery

Overview

The plugin connects to the same Pentair Cloud account used by the Pentair Pool phone app and automatically discovers supported connected equipment associated with the account.

IntelliFlo3 / IntelliFlo Pro3 VSF

Pentair device type IF31.

  • Online / connection status
  • Power
  • Motor speed
  • Flow
  • Pressure
  • Alarm status
  • Active program
  • Automatic discovery of configured pump programs
  • Start / Stop pump programs
  • Immediate Running / Idle feedback after commands

Each enabled pump program is discovered automatically and appears as its own IoX node.

Color Sync

Pentair device type PLC1.

  • Online / connection status
  • On / Off
  • Current color or light show
  • Red, White, Magenta, Green, Blue
  • Hold and Recall
  • SAm, Party, Romance, Caribbean, American, Sunset and Royal

Color Sync commands update IoX immediately after the Pentair command is accepted. Normal polling later confirms the actual device state.

IntelliCenter

The plugin automatically discovers supported equipment connected through Pentair IntelliCenter and creates the appropriate IoX nodes.

  • Automatic discovery of IntelliCenter equipment
  • Pool and spa body status and temperature
  • Pool and spa On / Off control
  • Heat setpoint control
  • Heat source selection
  • IntelliCenter pump status and control
  • Automatic discovery of pump circuit assignments
  • RPM / GPM mode selection and rate control for pump assignments
  • Heater status and control
  • IntelliChem pH, ORP, salt, alkalinity, calcium, and CYA monitoring
  • IntelliChem pH and ORP setpoint control
  • IntelliChlor salt level, output, and Super Chlorinate status
  • Auxiliary/circuit On / Off control
  • IntelliCenter color-light On / Off control
  • Color and light-show selection
  • Hold and Recall light commands

Configuration

Enter your Pentair Pool account credentials in the PG3x Custom Parameters:

ParameterValue
usernameYour Pentair account email address
passwordYour Pentair account password

After entering both values, click Save. The plugin will authenticate with Pentair Cloud and automatically discover supported equipment associated with the account.

Other Pentair Equipment

Pentair accounts may contain other connected device types. Unknown devices may be discovered and logged, but they are not added to IoX until support for that device type has been implemented and tested.

Security

Your Pentair username and password are used to authenticate with Pentair Cloud.

Important: Do not include your password, authentication tokens, or device identifiers in screenshots, forum posts, logs, or support requests.
